Spaghetti Carbonara - Step by Step

Narrator: "Today we are making an authentic Italian Spaghetti Carbonara. First, we boil a large pot of salted water."

Visual: A person fills a large stainless steel pot with water and adds a handful of sea salt. The water starts to bubble.

Narrator: "While the pasta cooks, we dice 150g of guanciale or pancetta and fry it until it's crispy and the fat has rendered out."

Visual: The chef carefully slices the meat on a wooden board, then tosses it into a hot pan. We hear and see the sizzling as the meat turns golden brown.

Narrator: "In a separate bowl, mix three large egg yolks with 50g of grated Pecorino Romano and plenty of black pepper."

Visual: Close-up shot of eggs being cracked, followed by a whisk mixing the bright yellow yolks with the finely grated cheese into a thick paste.

Narrator: "Finally, we combine everything. The heat from the pasta will cook the eggs into a creamy sauce. Remember: no cream allowed!"

Visual: The steaming spaghetti is tossed into the pan with the meat, then the egg mixture is poured over. The chef vigorously stirs the pasta until a silky, yellow sauce coats every strand. The final dish is served on a warm plate.
